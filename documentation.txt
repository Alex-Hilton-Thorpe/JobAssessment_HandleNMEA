3) NMEA Sentences - National Marine Electronics Association

- Standard to talk between electronics

  eg. $GPGLL,4916.45,N,12311.12,W,225444,A*10

5) GGA sentence - Global positioning system fix data

Reading GGA sentence:
$--GGA,hhmmss.ss,llll.ll,a,yyyy.yy,a,x,xx,x.x,x.x,M,x.x,M,x.x,xxxx*hh<CR><LF>

1) hhmmss.ss - UTC of position
2) llll.ll, a - Latitude, N/S (North/South)
3) yyyy.yy, a - Longitude, E/W (East/West)
4) x - GPS quality indicator (page 73)
5) xx - Number of satellites used (00-12), may differ from number in view
6) x.x - Horizontal dilution of precision (indicates accuracy based on satellite geometry, lower the better)
7) x.x - Antenna altitude, above/below (mean sea level)
8) M - units for 7)
9) x.x - Geoidal separation: Difference between the WGS-84 earth ellipsoid surface and mean sea level (if earth was perfectly smooth). If "-" then you have dropped below the perfect model.
10) M - units for 9)
11) x.x - Age of differential GPS data, time in seconds since last update
12) xxxx*hh<CR><LF> - Differential reference station ID, 0000-1023
    *hh = checksum (security)
